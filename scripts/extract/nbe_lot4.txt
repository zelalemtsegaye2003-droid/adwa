                                               Introduction

The accelerating adoption of digital financial services, core banking modernizations,
and automated interbank platforms has transformed the operational landscape of the
banking sector. Financial institutions leverage modern IT systems to deploy
innovative products, ensure seamless service delivery, and maintain financial
stability. However, this deep digital integration expands the attack surface, exposing
critical financial infrastructure to sophisticated cyber threats, extortion, and systemic
fraud. As the regulatory and supervisory authority of the nation's financial sector, the
National Bank of Ethiopia (NBE) must maintain an uncompromising cybersecurity
posture. Proactively identifying, evaluating, and mitigating system vulnerabilities is
vital to preserving institutional resilience and public confidence. Accordingly, this
procurement document sets forth the mandatory technical, operational, and
commercial requirements for an enterprise-grade Vulnerability Assessment and
Penetration Testing (VAPT) platform to safeguard the Bank's technological
ecosystem.

                                               Background
The National Bank of Ethiopia currently operates a complex, multi-tiered IT
infrastructure comprising enterprise data centers, core banking applications, interbank
payment systems, hybrid cloud assets, and widespread internal network segments. To
combat increasingly sophisticated threats and comply with regulatory security
mandates, the Bank must shift from periodic, manual security assessments to
continuous, automated vulnerability management and validated penetration testing
workflows.
Legacy assessment tools, siloed audit scripts, and uncoordinated scanning engines are
no longer sufficient to secure modern workloads against emerging attack vectors,
zero-day vulnerabilities, and credential compromise. To address these operational
gaps, the Bank requires a unified, modern VAPT solution that streamlines asset
discovery, provides dynamic risk prioritization based on active threat intelligence,
and verifies real-world exploitable pathways. This tender solicits competitive bids
from qualified vendors to supply, deploy, and support a comprehensive VAPT
platform aligned with international standards (such as ISO/IEC 27001, NIST CSF,
and CIS Benchmarks).

                                                    Scope
This tender encompasses the supply, licensing, architectural design, implementation,
and ongoing technical support of an enterprise-grade Vulnerability Assessment and
Penetration Testing (VAPT) software solution. The selected solution must deliver
automated, centralized, and credential-supported vulnerability evaluation across the
Bank’s heterogeneous IT environment without causing disruption to live production
services.
The scope of work covers:
    • Comprehensive Surface Coverage: Continuous assessment of all on-premises
       server estates, core networking equipment, perimeter firewalls, databases
       (SQL and NoSQL), Active Directory environments, virtualization clusters,
       endpoints, and external-facing digital interfaces.

     •    Web Application and API Security (DAST): Automated crawling,
          vulnerability scanning, and OWASP Top 10 auditing for internal and client-
          facing web applications, microservices, and REST/SOAP APIs.

     •    Exploitation Verification & Penetration Testing Tooling: Advanced
          capabilities to simulate attack paths, safely execute non-destructive exploit
          verifications, identify lateral movement vulnerabilities, and reduce false-
          positive triage time.

     •    Threat Prioritization & Exposure Scoring: Context-aware scoring engines
          factoring in CVSS metrics, the Exploit Prediction Scoring System (EPSS),
          CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV), and real-world weaponization
          data to prioritize urgent remediation workflows.

     •    Licensing, Appliance Deployment & Integration: Delivery of all necessary
          software licenses, management consoles, distributed virtual scan appliances,
          and seamless API integrations with the Bank’s existing SIEM systems.

     •    Knowledge Transfer & Warranty Support: Complete installation, vendor
          documentation, administrator and security analyst certification training,
          alongside a minimum three-year enterprise software subscription and 24/7
          technical support SLA.
